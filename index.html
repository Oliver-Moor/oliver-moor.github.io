Statistical Contraction Timer

ℹ️ How to Use (click to expand/collapse)

📱 Recording Contractions

Press the button when a contraction starts, then press again when it ends. The timer shows how long the current contraction has been going.

📊 Understanding the Stats

  • Last Duration: How long your most recent contraction lasted
  • Frequency: Time between the start of contractions
  • Averages: Mean contraction length over 30 min, 1 hour, or custom time
  • Standard Deviation: How consistent your contractions are (lower = more regular)

🎯 Statistical Prediction

The app calculates when your contractions might become perfectly regular by tracking how the standard deviation decreases over time. This "Singularity" prediction appears after you've logged enough contractions.

📈 Chart Views

  • Data Only: Just your contraction scatter plot
  • With Envelope: Shows convergence lines and prediction
  • Zoom to Convergence: Extended view to see where lines meet

⏱️ Custom Average Window

Enter any number of minutes to calculate the average for that specific time period. Perfect for answering your midwife's questions like "What's the average over the last 45 minutes?"

✏️ Editing & Exporting

Click Edit or Delete on any contraction in the history. Use Export CSV to save your data for your healthcare provider.
